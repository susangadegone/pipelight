# Pipelight demo script (≈2:30)

Read the **Say** lines aloud while doing the **Do** steps. Times are approximate.

---

**0:00 Hook**
- **Do:** Intake screen is open.
- **Say:** "Early-stage B2B teams find customers by stitching together a strategy deck, Google, Apollo, ChatGPT and a spreadsheet, and the context gets lost at every handoff. Pipelight is one engine that goes from a plain-English product description to cited leads, verified contacts, approved outreach and a tracked pipeline."

**0:15 Intake → ICP**
- **Do:** Click **Load example**, then **Infer ideal customer profile**.
- **Say:** "Here's a real product: an AI phone receptionist for dental clinics, with its existing customers and pricing. Claude infers the ideal customer profile: industries with NAICS codes, company size, buyer personas, and the trigger events that mean someone is ready to buy. Every field has a confidence score. Things I stated directly score high, and extrapolations score lower."

**0:40 Expansion segments**
- **Do:** Scroll to Expansion segments and click **Accept** on one.
- **Say:** "It also suggests adjacent markets I hadn't considered. I accept one, and it gets added to the search."

**0:50 Discovery with citations**
- **Do:** Click **Approve ICP & discover accounts** and point at the Agent Thinking panel.
- **Say:** "Now it searches the live web. The Agent Thinking panel shows the Planner running searches, the Builder extracting accounts, and the Reviewer enforcing our citation guard. Any lead that can't be traced to a page we actually retrieved is dropped. It also rejected the mega-DSOs, because they match my negative signals."

**1:10 Leads table**
- **Do:** Expand a Tier A row and hover a source link.
- **Say:** "Each account gets a fit-and-intent score and a tier, plus real buying signals like a new location opening on November 12th, each with its source link. Below that is the competitive landscape with public pricing and our edge over each competitor."

**1:25 Enrichment**
- **Do:** Click **Enrich top 5**.
- **Say:** "Hunter.io finds the decision-makers' business emails and verification status, ranked against my target personas."

**1:35 Graph**
- **Do:** Open **Graph** and click a company node.
- **Say:** "Everything lives in a knowledge graph: companies, people, signals and competitors, with typed relationships. It surfaces multi-hop patterns like accounts that share a buying signal and can go into one campaign."

**1:50 Outreach + approval gate**
- **Do:** On a lead, click **Draft**, open **Review**, edit a word, then click **Approve & send**.
- **Say:** "For each contact, Claude writes two email variants with different angles, plus a LinkedIn note. Each one opens with that company's real signal, shown as these chips. Nothing goes out without a human. I can edit, approve or reject. When I approve, it's dispatched and the lead moves to Contacted."

**2:15 Pipeline + analytics**
- **Do:** Open **Pipeline**, click **Simulate reply**, drag a card to **Meeting Booked**, then open **Analytics**.
- **Say:** "The pipeline shows exactly who moved each lead: purple for the AI, teal for the rep. Analytics breaks down conversion by industry and tier, shows A/B results per message angle, and keeps a full timestamped audit log of every AI and human action."

**2:30 Close**
- **Say:** "Pipelight: one context from ICP to closed deal, with every claim grounded in a source and every message approved by a human."
